Unleashing Creativity at The Heights Dance and Cheer Dance

Brightstars Classes

This 45 minute class builds confidence while exercising and having the best time while making forever friendships. Our All Abilites classes cater for 16+ years and we aim to keep the timetable the same year after year as we understand that changing routines can be tricky.

Preschool Classes

Little Movers: Jazz, Tap, Acro and Ballet. Join in for a world of creativity and fun! Our Preschoolers know how to have fun and we love it.

Starting off with Ballet to help our little loves learn dance foundations, Next is tap which is great for isolating ankle joints, Next is Acro which teaches the basics like bunny hops and forward rolls and don’t forget about the fun obstacle courses, Lastly we have Jazz for fun moving and grooving to the best songs we know they all love.

Competition Classes

At The Heights Dance and Cheer, we offer the following genres to compete in All Star competitions.

  • Jazz
  • Lyrical
  • Pom
  • Hip Hop
  • Cheer

These classes have a high level of commitment and require a strong class ethic.

We strive for upskilling while having a sense of belonging within each team. It’s not about coming first all the time (while that is always very exciting), its about being the best version of yourself while reaching your peak!

Recreational Classes

Did somebody say FUN??? Please read on…

Our Recreational classes are 45mins and have no performance commitment. We offer an end of year concert to perform in and showoff what was learnt all year but thats it! So if you are a busy parent and your child loves to dance then these classes are for you.

While we learn technique and dance combinations throughout class, we also make sure our students have a great time while making friends along the way.

Private Lessons and Duos

At The Heights Dance and Cheer we offer a variety of Private Lessons and Duo classes.

Whether your child would love to perform with a friend, just the two of them OR maybe your child is such a confident superstar who can own the stage alone, We support that!

It’s your choice how often they compete, if at all, Big competitions, Little competitions, we love them all.

Exam Classes

We offer Glenwood Tap Exams and RAD Ballet Exams.
